First, the gist of this week is to *solve differential equations*, and why do we want to solve differential equations?
Because we want to analyze *time-variant linear systems*. Linear here has a precise definition.
Mathematically, it means all differentials in the ODEs are of order 1 (e.g. siny,$\frac{ydy}{dx}$ are not linear).
Intuitively, it means when we give the systems two inputs, the response would be the same as the sum of the two same but separate responses.

We learnt three types of linear equations.
## First-order
solve method: 
$$
\frac{d}{dt}y+yf(t)=a(t)
$$

$$

$$\begin{align}

$$\begin{align}
