# Emergency Portfolio Note — SpaceX Spectrum Acquisition Agreement

*10 October 2026 | Latest SPCX quote: $162.57 (Friday close, 9 October); transaction remains subject to approval*

## What happened

On 8 October, SpaceX announced an agreement to acquire Grain Management's nationwide 800 MHz low-band spectrum portfolio, comprising up to 14 MHz of paired spectrum. SpaceX says the low band would complement Starlink Mobile's 2 GHz mid-band spectrum, improve signal penetration indoors and support a hybrid satellite/terrestrial mobile network. The company states that deployment is subject to final FCC approval. [SpaceX announcement](https://www.spacex.com/updates/starship-moon-announcement), [Grain Management transaction announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/grain-management-announces-definitive-agreement-to-sell-nationwide-800-mhz-spectrum-portfolio-to-spacex-302902974.html)

The parties have not disclosed the transaction price in their announcements. A reported ~$8 billion figure is a media estimate, not a company-confirmed term; it should not be used as a confirmed valuation or financing input.

## Expected-value and risk implications

The agreement adds option value if SpaceX can combine satellite coverage, low-band indoor penetration and terrestrial capacity into a differentiated mobile product. It may improve addressable market and reduce reliance on third-party mobile-network partners.

However, the current thesis card already flags heavy capital expenditure, AI and Starship funding demands, governance/capital-allocation risk and a valuation rating of **0/10** near the card's 28 September reference price. A nationwide terrestrial network could require substantial spectrum, site, equipment, backhaul, customer-acquisition and compliance spending. The transaction therefore can increase long-term connectivity upside while also increasing capital requirements and competing with other high-return investment priorities. Without a disclosed price, financing plan, FCC approval and deployment economics, no defensible numerical change to per-share expected value or the 12-month rating can be made.

## Catalyst and monitoring checklist

1. **FCC license-transfer approval:** confirm the regulatory scope, conditions and timing before treating the spectrum as available for deployment.
2. **Transaction economics:** disclosed consideration, financing, any equity issuance/debt, spectrum liabilities and closing conditions.
3. **Network plan:** whether SpaceX will build terrestrial infrastructure itself or use partners; deployment sequence, coverage and launch timing.
4. **Operating proof:** Starlink Mobile device compatibility, subscriber uptake, service quality indoors, spectrum utilization and incremental capex/opex.
5. **Quarterly KPIs:** Connectivity revenue and operating margin; Starlink users and ARPU; mobile-specific subscribers/revenue if reported; total capex and free-cash-flow conversion; AI and Starship funding requirements.
