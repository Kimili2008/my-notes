[Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/micron-pauses-work-on-45-billion-taiwan-plant-bloomberg-news-reports-5002732) said it could not independently verify the report. Micron's statement quoted by Bloomberg says **current Taiwan operations continue normally** and that the company is confident in customer continuity; Micron did not directly confirm the planned plant's status. Therefore classify the *expansion pause* as **reported, not issuer-confirmed**, and do **not** describe existing production as halted.
2. **The sector is selling off together.** The sampled quotes show MU −12.39%, SNDK −17.09%, SOXX −7.78%, NVDA −6.52% and AVGO −6.30%. This supports a broad risk/valuation component; it does not isolate a single causal driver.
