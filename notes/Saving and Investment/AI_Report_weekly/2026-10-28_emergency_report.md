## Investment conclusion

SoFi's 27 October Q3 release supports the card's central claim that member growth is converting into deeper product relationships, fee revenue and profit. Adjusted net revenue rose **44% year over year to $1.371 billion**; adjusted EBITDA rose **70% to $469.8 million**; GAAP diluted EPS was **$0.18**, versus $0.11 a year earlier. Management raised 2026 adjusted revenue guidance to **$5.04 billion** and introduced 2027 guidance of **$6.4 billion** adjusted revenue and **$0.90** EPS. The shares' latest 27 October quote was approximately **$16.40, up 13.38%** from the previous close. These are meaningful results and a post-earnings decision point, not a reason to mechanically chase a one-day rally. ## Expected-value and risk implications

The existing September SOFI card used **$1.05 normalized FY2027 EPS** in its base case and a **$19.6–20.6 risk-adjusted target range** from a $16.58 reference price. Management's newly published **$0.90 FY2027 EPS outlook is 14% below that card's base-case EPS**. These are not necessarily identical accounting/normalization definitions, so the difference is a re-underwriting prompt, not a direct 14% target-price cut. At a $16.40 quote, the share price is about **18.2× management's 2027 EPS outlook**; whether that is attractive depends on the durability of 27% guided adjusted-revenue growth, credit losses and share count. Do **not** simply carry forward the card's old target or upgrade its +2/10 rating because the quarter was strong.

One offset to the strong per-share story: period-end common shares outstanding rose from **1.205 billion to 1.297 billion** year on year (approximately **7.6%**). GAAP diluted weighted-average shares also rose. Monitor whether per-share earnings growth continues to outpace dilution. The card's other explicit breakers—two quarters of total net charge-offs above 3% and rising, sustained NIM decline, fee share below 30%, or renewed large technology-client losses—are **not established by this release**. [SoFi Q3 share-count and credit tables](https://investors.sofi.com/news/news-details/2026/SoFi-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-with-Record-Net-Revenue-of-1-4-Billion-Net-Income-of-241-Million-and-Raises-Full-Year-Guidance/default.aspx)

## Conditional action framework and next catalysts

- **If** subsequent quarters sustain product growth ahead of member growth, fee share above 40%, NIM stability and improving *comparable* credit measures, the operating thesis remains reinforced; revisit the valuation model and position size against the new $0.90 EPS guide before considering an addition.
- **If** late-stage delinquent-loan sales mask deterioration in underlying loss rates, third-party Loan Platform demand slows, or dilution persistently outruns per-share earnings, reduce the growth multiple assumed in the card and consider a smaller risk budget.
- **If** the card's comparable, explicitly defined breakers are met for the required duration, reassess the thesis independent of the entry price. Do not substitute the 3.4% estimated *personal-loan* all-in charge-off measure for its *total* charge-off threshold.

Next evidence gates: SoFi's Q3 10-Q and any detailed credit/vintage disclosures; Q4 member/product cross-sell, deposit cost, NIM, fee share, Loan Platform funding and Technology Platform retention; progress against management's 2027 EPS outlook. No leveraged or short-trading instruction is implied.
