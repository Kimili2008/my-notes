## Executive assessment

NVIDIA's independent directors confirmed on 10 November that they retained former acting U.S. Attorney Joon H. Kim and Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz for an independent investigation. This follows the board's 8 November statement that it is examining reporting about a foreign intelligence effort targeting CEO Jensen Huang. The board said it had **no reason to believe national security or company secrets were compromised**, expressed confidence in Huang and said he was cooperating. These are the company's current statements, not findings from the investigation. The new appointment makes the review more concrete, but neither misconduct nor a regulatory sanction has been established. [NVIDIA's 10 November statement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-the-independent-members-of-the-nvidia-board); [8 November board statement](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/08/3398017/0/en/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors.html).

This reinforces the interest-rate sensitivity of the portfolio's concentrated growth and semiconductor exposure; it does not prove a change in NVIDIA's customer orders. BLS could not calculate most October year-on-year rates because October 2025 survey data were not collected. The combination of a governance review, a hotter-than-desired inflation path and earnings on **17 November** creates a nearer-term decision point. ## Thesis and expected-value implications

The event introduces three scenario branches absent from the old NVDA card: **(1)** an investigation that finds no compromise and leaves operations intact; **(2)** a prolonged governance overhang that raises the discount rate or limits export permissions without changing near-term orders; and **(3)** adverse findings or a leadership change that could impair customer, regulator or execution confidence. Only the first branch is consistent with the board's current reassurance; the other branches remain contingent. A 5.5% share-price decline alone does not establish a cheaper risk-adjusted entry point or a broken AI platform moat.

A defensible numerical expected-value revision requires the investigation's findings, any regulatory action, updated data-center orders and margins, and an explicit probability and duration for each governance scenario. Those inputs are unavailable. Mark the previous price target and rating **under review** rather than changing them mechanically. October CPI raises the valuation hurdle for long-duration growth, but cannot be mapped to a precise NVDA target from one data point. [NVIDIA board statement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-the-independent-members-of-the-nvidia-board); [BLS CPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_11102026.htm).
