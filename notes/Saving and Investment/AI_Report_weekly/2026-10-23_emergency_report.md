# Event Alert | SpaceX: SI Customer Concentration Rises Sharply

**23 October 2026 | SPCX | Thesis status: Core operations reinforced; SI revenue quality under review**

## Investment conclusion

SpaceX's 22 October post-close Form 10-Q adds a material qualification to the strong Q3 operating results discussed in the [21 October alert](2026-10-21_emergency_report.md): one anonymous SI customer (“Customer B”) generated **34.5% of consolidated Q3 revenue**, versus **19.5% in Q2**. This is an exposure to a single counterparty within the capital-intensive business on which much of the equity's incremental value depends. There is **no disclosed cancellation, non-payment, or contract impairment**; the development raises the downside variance of the SI thesis rather than proving that it is broken. The 22 October share-lockup alert addresses a separate supply/price risk and should not be conflated with this filing-based fundamental risk. [Q3 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026066880/spcx-20260930.htm); [Q2 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026052535/spcx-20260630.htm).

## What the filing changes

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q3 2026 | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Customer B share of consolidated revenue | 19.5% | **34.5%** | Concentration rose 15 percentage points; the filing attributes this customer's revenue to SI. |
| Implied Customer B quarterly revenue | ~$1.52bn | **~$5.03bn** | Analyst calculation: disclosed customer share × quarterly group revenue; not separately reported contract value. |
| Customer B as a share of SI revenue | ~59% | **~67%** | Analyst calculation using Q2 SI revenue of $2.561bn and Q3 SI revenue of $7.560bn. |
| Backlog | $47.461bn | **$62.959bn** | Better contracted-revenue visibility, but the filing does not disclose the backlog's customer mix. |

At 30 September, one unnamed customer also represented **27.7% of net accounts receivable**, compared with no customer at or above 10% at year-end 2025. The filing does **not** say whether that receivables customer is Customer B; it would be unsound to assume so. Q3 group revenue was $14.575bn; SI revenue was $7.560bn and SI operating income was $431mn. The Q3 10-Q was accepted by the SEC on **22 October at 16:03 ET**, after the regular session, so the filing cannot be assumed to have caused the regular-session 22 October stock decline. [Q3 SEC filing index](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026066880/0001628280-26-066880-index.htm); [Q3 results release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026066482/spaceexplorationtechnologi.htm); [Q2 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026052535/spcx-20260630.htm).

## Thesis and expected-value implications

The user's [SPCX thesis card](SPCX_2026-09-28.md) already identifies AI contract quality, utilization, renewals, capex, and cash returns as critical variables. Q3's move into SI operating profit and larger backlog support the demand and near-term execution legs. Yet ~two-thirds of SI quarterly revenue being attributable to one customer means a contract repricing, utilization reduction, payment delay, or non-renewal could materially change the segment's economics while capex remains committed. The card's September **$160–175 risk-adjusted value** predates Q3 and this concentration disclosure and should not be treated as a refreshed target. Without customer identity, duration, minimum take-or-pay commitments, prepayments, cancellation provisions, contract margins, and backlog concentration, a defensible new point estimate of expected value is unavailable. The proper adjustment now is **higher downside-scenario weight / a wider value range**, not a fabricated target-price revision. [Q3 SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026066880/spcx-20260930.htm).

## Portfolio materiality and conditional decision framework

The current [portfolio file](../Portfolio_AI.md) lists **7.34 SPCX shares**, but cash, other assets, cost bases, and total account value are absent. Therefore an exact account-level impact cannot be verified. For scale, a 15% SPCX move would affect a 13%-weight equity sleeve by about **1.95%** before other holdings move; this is a scenario, **not** a forecast or a statement of the user's full-account weight. The filing is alert-worthy primarily because it materially changes the written SI risk assessment, not because today's account loss can be established.

- **Hold thesis intact, monitor more closely** if the next filing supports durable contracted utilization, timely collections, and broader SI customer diversification while SI operating profit and cash returns improve.
- **Re-underwrite before adding** if management does not clarify the nature of the concentrated exposure; a lower share price or lockup-related selling alone does not remove counterparty risk.
- **Consider a smaller risk budget** if the large customer reduces commitments, payment quality deteriorates, SI margin reverses while capex remains elevated, or the exposure appears related-party/otherwise weaker than arm's-length demand. These are conditions for reassessment, not an instruction to trade today.

**Next checkpoints:** any company clarification of customer/contract concentration; future 10-Q receivables and SI-margin disclosures; the next lockup tranche expected to become tradable on 26 October (a separate technical catalyst); and the next quarterly SI capex, operating cash flow, backlog mix, and renewal evidence. No leverage or short positioning is suggested.
