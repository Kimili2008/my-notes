A broad simultaneous repricing of AI and memory stocks is evident from prices, but available primary disclosures do not establish that Friday's full move reflects canceled orders or an already broken MU/SNDK thesis. Do not convert unconfirmed shipment or NAND-pricing market commentary into issuer fact.

**Expected value:** prior card target prices are scenario assumptions, not live fair-value estimates. Lower prices raise arithmetic upside only if cash-flow, cycle and legal-risk assumptions hold. The new regulatory uncertainty and sharp multiple compression require wider downside scenarios and a fresh assessment of normalized memory margins; no defensible revised expected value is available from the current evidence alone.
