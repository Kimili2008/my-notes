## New information and thesis read-through

1. **Export controls and AI demand risk.** The [Bureau of Industry and Security's 19 November interim final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/11/19/2026-23529/addressing-remote-access-to-advanced-semiconductors) adds restrictions on certain overseas data-center access to advanced US computing technology and license requirements for advanced semiconductor transfers to specified countries, subject to exceptions. This increases uncertainty around eligible markets, customer diligence and deployment timing for NVDA and AVGO, with potential second-order effects on AI infrastructure demand. It does **not** establish that MU or SNDK has lost orders. **Thesis implication:** AI demand estimates need geography- and customer-level stress testing; no blanket thesis break follows from the rule alone.

2. **NVIDIA investigation.** On 19 November the [California Attorney General said it had served an investigative subpoena on NVIDIA](https://oag.ca.gov/node/633133) about possible chip/confidential-information transfers and investor disclosures. These are questions under investigation, **not findings of misconduct**. Governance, export-compliance and legal-disclosure risk now warrant explicit downside scenarios for NVDA and, through sentiment, AI peers. The issuer's [fiscal Q3 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27pr.htm) reported revenue of **$115.8 billion** (+103% year on year), Data Center revenue of **$107.6 billion** (+110%) and fiscal Q4 revenue guidance of **$130 billion ±3%**, alongside a lower guided non-GAAP gross-margin band of **71–72%** versus Q3's **74.9%**. Operating demand was strong in the reported quarter; valuation and legal-risk assumptions are under review. No conclusion about the investigation can yet be drawn.

3. **SNDK stock split, plus an actual price fall.** Sandisk's [19 November 8-K](https://investor.sandisk.com/node/8536/html) confirms its **8-for-1 forward split** and split-adjusted trading from today. The portfolio's **1.8 pre-split shares imply 14.4 post-split shares**, subject to broker processing and any intervening trade. The latest [company results](https://investor.sandisk.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sandisk-reports-fiscal-first-quarter-2027-financial-results) showed strong revenue and forward guidance, but the NAND cycle, inventory and cash conversion remain the decisive tests.
