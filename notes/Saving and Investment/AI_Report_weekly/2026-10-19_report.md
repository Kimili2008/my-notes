Energy contributed heavily, so the headline prints should not be projected linearly into all firms' costs. [BLS CPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm); [BLS PPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ppi.nr0.htm); [BLS import/export prices](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ximpim.nr0.htm).

Falling nominal yields alongside hot price data may reflect growth/credit concerns or flight to quality; that interpretation is an inference, not a confirmed cause. It does not remove valuation risk if inflation persists. [U.S. Treasury daily yields](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value=2026&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve).

Treat supply discipline as a watch item, not a proven thesis break. [AP Friday market recap](https://apnews.com/article/c27809783ae3230b44c26e5ee4bea056); [Reuters sector report](https://au.marketscreener.com/news/us-chip-stocks-extend-slide-as-china-memory-report-ai-spending-jitters-hit-sector-ce785dddd18ff02c).
