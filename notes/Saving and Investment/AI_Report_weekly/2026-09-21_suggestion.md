## Recommendation in One Sentence

**Maintain a growth-oriented portfolio, but reduce single-factor AI concentration and keep 15–20% liquid reserves until Micron's 30 September earnings and the rate backdrop provide a cleaner risk/reward entry point.**

## Suggested Target Framework

| Sleeve | Target range | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Liquidity / SGOV | **15–20%** | Dry powder and volatility control in a rising-yield regime |
| Core AI infrastructure (AVGO + NVDA + MU combined) | **25–35%** | High-conviction earnings exposure, but treated as one correlated factor bucket |
| Profitable platforms (META + AMZN + optional UBER) | **25–35%** | More diversified revenue engines and stronger cash-flow support |
| International / high-growth satellite (GRAB + other speculative names) | **5–10%** | Keeps upside optionality while respecting execution risk |
| Defensive diversifiers / non-AI exposure | **10–20%** | Reduces dependence on one capex and discount-rate regime |
| Crypto | **0–10%** | Maintain only if volatility budget and liquidity need allow it |

These ranges are deliberately less aggressive than a 40% annual-return objective. A 40% target generally requires leverage, concentration or both; those choices can produce losses that make the target mathematically harder to recover. The portfolio should earn the right to add risk through validated thesis milestones.

## Security-Level Actions

### AVGO — Hold; add only below $350 or on diversification evidence

- **Action:** Hold core exposure. Do not increase simply because the latest earnings were strong.
- **Add condition:** A valuation reset toward / below $350 **or** clear evidence that AI revenue is broadening beyond a small customer set.
- **Reduce condition:** Material reduction in the $115bn AI revenue ambition, a customer-project delay, or FCF margin below 30%.
- **Sizing guardrail:** Avoid allowing AVGO to exceed 10–12% of total portfolio value without a separate concentration decision.

### MU — Hold / staged add; no leverage before 30 September

- **Action:** Retain if already held; any additional buying should be staged rather than front-loaded.
- **Add condition:** Earnings validate HBM ramp, pricing and 2027 demand while management remains disciplined on capacity; a post-event dislocation with intact guidance is preferable to a pre-event bet.
- **Reduce condition:** Simultaneous deterioration in DRAM pricing and HBM orders, accelerated competitive supply, or an adverse 2027 margin / capex outlook.
- **Sizing guardrail:** Keep at 5–8% before the earnings release; do not use borrowed exposure for this catalyst.

### NVDA — Hold; define the missing thesis card before adding

- **Action:** Retain core holding if its weight is within the AI-infrastructure sleeve limit.
- **Add condition:** Written valuation / earnings case confirms adequate upside and the combined AVGO + MU + NVDA weight remains ≤35%.
- **Reduce condition:** Hyperscaler capex cuts, material competitive share loss, or the stock moving materially above the documented fair-value range without an estimate revision.

### META and AMZN — Prefer incremental capital here over additional semiconductor beta

- **Action:** Hold; use as the first destination for capital reallocated from excessive chip concentration.
- **Reason:** Both combine AI upside with large, diversified operating platforms and cash generation.
- **Required next step:** Create a thesis card for each—capex, monetisation, margin and fair-value ranges—before increasing beyond a normal core weight.

### GRAB — Small staged add only; cap at satellite size

- **Action:** The $2.795 price meets the thesis card's stated strong-buy threshold, but the stock should remain a 3–5% satellite rather than a core holding.
- **Add condition:** Growth remains ≥20%, financial services accelerates and credit losses remain controlled.
- **Stop / reduce condition:** Two consecutive quarters below 20% revenue growth, deteriorating margin, or rising credit stress.

### UBER — Candidate for a 3–5% starter position, if not already owned

- **Action:** The current $70.50 share price is within the thesis card's buy band. Establish only a starter position, funded from excess semiconductor concentration or cash—not leverage.
- **Why:** Core cash-flow and delivery economics are already established; recent AV launches reinforce the strategic option value.
- **Stop / reduce condition:** Gross bookings below 10% growth, margin stagnation, FCF deterioration, or AV partners bypassing Uber's consumer / dispatch layer.

## What Would Change This Recommendation

- A material decline in Treasury yields driven by easing inflation could justify a higher growth allocation.
- Strong MU guidance with confirmed supply discipline could support increasing the AI-infrastructure sleeve within its cap.
- A break in AI capex, renewed inflation upside, or higher-for-longer Fed guidance should increase the liquidity and defensive sleeves.
