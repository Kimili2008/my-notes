## Holdings and thesis review

### AI semiconductors — 42.5%

- **AVGO — Reinforced; +2/10 card rating.** Custom silicon and networking exposure remain well positioned for AI infrastructure demand. At 13.0% of disclosed equity value and alongside NVDA/MU, additions should be contingent on valuation support, continued AI revenue growth and customer diversification—not on sector momentum alone.
- **NVDA — Intact; +3/10.** The demand/backlog case remains strong, but valuation depends on hyperscaler spending continuing and on margins holding amid product transitions, export controls and customer ASIC substitution. Monitor data-center growth, gross margin, supply commitments and customer concentration.
- **MU — Reinforced; +3/10.** The October 1 card update incorporates record FY2026 results and strong FY2027 Q1 guidance. Near-term HBM/DRAM pricing and supply discipline are supportive; peak-cycle earnings, customer concentration and rapid memory supply response remain the principal risks. At 17.2%, the position alone is larger than the entire disclosed APP+AMZN allocation; avoid extrapolating one quarter's margins into terminal earnings. [Micron FY2026 results](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Record-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2026-Results/default.aspx)

### Platforms, cloud and infrastructure

- **APP — Intact, under review; +2/10.** The October 1 card update flags Q2 Axon model uplift below its usual level, third-party concerns about MAX share-of-wallet and e-commerce lead quality, and higher compute expense. Q3 management guidance still implies sequential growth and roughly 83% adjusted EBITDA margin, so the evidence is mixed, not thesis-breaking. Verify revenue/EBITDA against guidance, model improvement, high-quality e-commerce advertiser growth, publisher economics and margin. Do not average down solely because the share price has declined. [APP Q2 results and Q3 guidance](https://investors.applovin.com/news/news-details/2026/AppLovin-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/)
- **ROAD — Intact; +1/10.** At $86.81, the quote is below the card's September reference; lower price alone is not proof of undervaluation. Backlog conversion, E-Infrastructure margins, organic growth, cash conversion and leverage following acquisitions remain the key tests.
- **AMZN — Intact/reinforced; +4/10.** AWS growth and advertising remain the core value drivers. The critical test is whether AI and logistics capex translate into durable AWS operating profit and improving free cash flow. At 2.8%, a measured build can be researched only after total-account cash needs and risk capacity are known.

### Space — 22.7%

- **SPCX — Intact; 0/10.** SpaceX reported two Starlink Mobile milestones on 8 October: agreement to acquire a nationwide portfolio of up to 14 MHz paired 800 MHz spectrum, subject to final FCC approval, and FCC approval of the Gen2 constellation application for 15,000 satellites optimized for 2 GHz. The latter reduces one regulatory/constellation uncertainty and supports capacity expansion; it does **not** approve the separate transfer of the 800 MHz licenses. Financial terms and incremental returns were not disclosed. Monitor FCC transfer approval, deployment, indoor coverage performance, capex, subscriber adoption and Starlink margins. [SpaceX company update](https://www.spacex.com/updates/starship-2025-report)
- **RKLB — Intact; 0/10.** The 20-mission Synspective Electron order extends backlog visibility into 2028–31, but the contract value is undisclosed and the cash/margin contribution is long-dated. Neutron first-flight timing, launch cadence, gross margin and financing/dilution remain central.
