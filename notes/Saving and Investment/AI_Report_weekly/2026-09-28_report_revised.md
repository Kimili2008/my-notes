## Executive view

The portfolio is fundamentally oriented toward secular growth, but its risk is materially more concentrated than the share count suggests. Marking the disclosed positions to the latest available quotes produces an estimated gross equity value of **$9,431.76**, excluding cash, taxes, fees and any unlisted assets. Three AI-semiconductor holdings (AVGO, NVDA and MU) represent approximately **43.0%** of marked value; SPCX and RKLB add approximately **22.6%** of high-duration space exposure. The portfolio therefore has a strong upside engine, but it is also exposed to one common factor: long-duration growth expectations and sustained AI capital expenditure.

The written theses are mostly **reinforced or intact**, not broken. The principal near-term risk is not a company-specific collapse; it is multiple compression if rates and oil remain elevated, or if AI demand is merely delayed. The most important imminent catalyst is **Micron's FY2026 Q4 release on 30 September after the close**. Given the position size and the cycle sensitivity of memory earnings, this is a genuine decision point rather than routine news.

## Thesis review and expected-value implications

### AI semiconductors — AVGO, NVDA, MU (43.0% combined)

Broadcom's Q3 FY2026 revenue was $29.6bn (+86%), AI semiconductor revenue was $16.7bn (+221%), and Q4 guidance was $34.8bn revenue with $21.7bn of AI semiconductor revenue. This reinforces the ASIC/networking thesis, but at roughly $353 the card still assigns only a +2/10 valuation rating and a 15–25% risk-adjusted upside. **Hold; add only on a thesis-consistent pullback or further guide-up, not simply because the stock is volatile.** [Broadcom Q3 FY2026 release](https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/broadcom-inc-announces-third-quarter-fiscal-year-2026-financial)

NVIDIA remains the highest-quality AI infrastructure asset in the book. The card describes data-centre growth above 100%, roughly 75% gross margins and a very high forward revenue bar. The thesis is **intact**, but the portfolio already has substantial correlated exposure through AVGO and MU. **Hold; cap incremental AI exposure until the combined bucket is closer to a deliberate ceiling.**

Micron's operational thesis is reinforced by Q3 revenue of $41.46bn, non-GAAP EPS of $25.11 and roughly 84.9% gross margin. However, memory is cyclical and the current valuation may be using peak earnings. The company guides to approximately $50bn Q4 revenue, 86% gross margin and $31 ± $1 non-GAAP EPS. **Under review until the release confirms demand, pricing and HBM supply discipline.** [Micron events and results](https://investors.micron.com/events-and-presentations/default.aspx)

### Platforms and infrastructure — APP, ROAD, AMZN

APP's advertising model remains structurally attractive and the card's +3/10 rating reflects meaningful but volatile upside. **Small position; hold and let KPI delivery, not price momentum, earn a larger weight.**

ROAD reported roughly $999.4m Q3 revenue (+28%), $59.6m net income (+35%), $3.36bn backlog and FY26 revenue guidance of $3.64–3.68bn. The card rates it only +1/10 because the shares are near fair value after allowing for execution and capital intensity. **Hold; add only if backlog conversion and margins improve without leverage creep.** [ROAD Q3 FY26 results](https://ir.constructionpartners.net/news/press-releases/detail/163/construction-partners-inc-announces-fiscal-2026-third-quarter-results)

AMZN remains the portfolio's clearest quality-at-a-reasonable-price exposure: Q2 sales grew about 20%, AWS about 37% and AWS operating profit was approximately $16.6bn. TTM free cash flow was negative as AI and logistics capex rose. **Reinforced, but monitor capex returns and FCF conversion; the small weight is a portfolio-underexposure, not a reason to chase.**

### Space — SPCX and RKLB (22.6% combined)

SPCX's Q2 revenue was reported at $7.8bn (+92%); Connectivity generated $4.3bn revenue and $1.7bn operating profit, while AI generated $2.6bn revenue but a $1.3bn operating loss. The card's final rating is 0/10: Starlink quality is high, but AI and Starship success are already heavily capitalised. Starship Flight 14 was targeted for 28 September subject to regulatory approval. **Hold, but do not increase the combined space bucket ahead of binary catalysts.**

RKLB's Space Systems revenue represented approximately 81% of sales and grew 94%; backlog reached $2.36bn (+137%). The revised card also flags weaker near-term margin guidance, the Iridium acquisition and ATM financing. **Intact but under review; treat dilution and Neutron execution as explicit valuation inputs.** [Rocket Lab investor news](https://investors.rocketlabcorp.com/news?_correlationId=1&page=0)

### Financial growth — NU and SOFI (18.2% combined)

NU continues to show strong customer and ROE economics, but 90+ day delinquency is rising and Brazil remains a concentration. Its +3/10 rating supports a **staged-hold/add-on-weakness** posture rather than a one-shot increase. SoFi's member growth, cross-sell and fee mix are improving; its +2/10 rating and 15–25% risk-adjusted upside imply **hold**, with credit losses and Technology Platform retention as the principal falsification tests. [SoFi investor releases](https://investors.sofi.com/news/Press-Releases/)

## Bottom line

The portfolio's long-run growth thesis is still credible, but the current construction is more aggressive than the stated desire for risk control suggests. The best improvement is **concentration management**, not wholesale thesis replacement: keep the strongest compounders, make new capital conditional on valuation and KPI confirmation, and use the MU result plus the payrolls report to reassess the common AI-duration factor.

This is research, not a trade instruction. It does not recommend leverage or short positions.
