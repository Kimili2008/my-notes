## Investment committee conclusion

NVIDIA's **17 November results materially reinforce the operating-demand leg** of the written thesis: fiscal Q3 revenue was **$115.8 billion (+103% year on year)**, Data Center revenue **$107.6 billion (+110%)**, non-GAAP gross margin **74.9%**, and free cash flow **$43.8 billion**. Rubin shipments have begun. Fiscal Q4 revenue guidance is **$130 billion ±3%**, with **no Data Center compute revenue from China assumed**. These figures do not meet the card's demand or sustained-sub-70% gross-margin breakers. [Earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27pr.htm); [CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm); [NVDA thesis card](../stocks%20Evaluation/NVDA_2026-09-28.md).

The **investment case remains under review** because Q4 guided gross margin steps down to **71–72%**, receivable days increased, and capital/supply commitments expanded. The $118 billion maximum **gross contingent guarantee exposure** is especially important to value separately from recognized debt; management says no payments have yet been made under those guarantees. The abrupt CEO transition and the new BIS rule add governance and licensing uncertainty. A strong revenue quarter does not settle those risks. [CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm); [CEO transition](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-CEO-Transition/); [BIS rule](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf).

## Evidence bridge against the card

| Written test | New observation | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Data Center growth below 40% for two quarters | **+110%** year on year this quarter | **Reinforced**, far from breaker |
| Gross margin persistently below 70% | Q3 **74.9%** non-GAAP; Q4 guide **71–72%** | **Not broken**, but direction needs explanation |
| Cloud customer demand and product roadmap | Data Center +21% sequential; Rubin shipping | **Reinforced**, subject to order/collection quality |
| Receivables and cash conversion | Receivables **$81.0bn**, DSO **64 days versus 60** sequential; Q3 FCF **$43.8bn** | **Mixed**: strong cash generation, slower collections |
| Supply and data-center guarantees | Supply commitments **$390bn versus $279bn** last quarter; cloud/lease commitments **$102bn versus $74bn**; maximum gross contingent guarantees **$118bn** | **Risk-up**: long-dated obligations and counterparty exposure need stress testing |
| China/export controls | Q3 Hopper Data Center shipments to China **$2.4bn**; Q4 guide assumes **zero** Data Center compute revenue from China | **Under review**: baseline already excludes China sales; new rule can affect third-country routes |

All numbers and management explanations in this table are from the [SEC-furnished CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm), except the card's stated tests, which come from the [current thesis card](../stocks%20Evaluation/NVDA_2026-09-28.md). The 10-Q is expected later, so the final filed risk and cash-flow detail may differ.

The Q4 guide includes an **additional week**, so a simple $130bn/$115.8bn comparison overstates like-for-like sequential growth. Gross margin guidance is **2.9–3.9 percentage points below** Q3's 74.9%; at $130bn revenue, each one-point change is roughly **$1.3bn of quarterly gross profit** before operating costs and taxes. This is sensitivity arithmetic, not a forecast that the entire margin difference is permanent. Management attributes the recent margin pattern partly to the Rubin ramp, but pricing, mix and supply economics must be tested in subsequent quarters. [Earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27pr.htm); [CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm).

## Conditional action framework and next catalysts

1. **Separate operations from risk transfer.** The operating thesis is reinforced; portfolio concentration and contingent financing exposure still warrant review. Do not equate a strong headline revenue result with a risk-free add signal.
2. **Reconcile account risk first.** Determine NVDA and combined MU/SNDK/AVGO/NVDA weights across the *entire* brokerage account, including cash, and set a correlated-loss budget. A 10% simultaneous move in the four names is approximately **5.7% of the disclosed sleeve**, before offsets; whole-account impact is unknown.
3. **Demand the missing bridge.** In the next company filing/call, track Rubin gross margin as the ramp matures; Q4 revenue adjusted for the extra week; DSO, receivables and FCF; supplier and neocloud/customer commitments; guarantees actually becoming effective or paid; and the permanent-CEO process. The public CFO commentary says maximum guarantee exposure builds over time and that no payments have yet been made. [CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000088/q3fy27cfocommentary.htm).
4. **Near-term calendar.** Verify the BIS rule upon its expected **19 November** publication; ROAD's final FY2026 results/call are **19 November**; SNDK starts split-adjusted trading **20 November**. [BIS public-inspection text](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf); [ROAD calendar](https://ir.constructionpartners.net/events-presentations); [SNDK release](https://investor.sandisk.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sandisk-reports-fiscal-first-quarter-2027-financial-results).
