## Event 1 — NU / possible Monzo acquisition

Axios reported that Nu has held talks regarding a possible acquisition of Monzo, with a potential valuation of £8–10bn. Other market coverage attributed the sharp sell-off to concerns about paying a premium, integration complexity and possible cash/share dilution. This is currently **reported discussion, not a completed deal**.

Sources: [Axios report](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/28/nu-monzo-acquisition-talks), [Investing.com market reaction](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/why-is-nu-holdings-stock-sliding-today-93CH-4920607), [Nu official newsroom](https://nu.com/en/newsroom).

### Thesis impact

The written NU thesis assumes strong customer growth, high ROE and a staged approach because credit risk is already meaningful. A large cross-border acquisition would add a new risk vector: capital allocation and management bandwidth. It would not automatically break the operating thesis, but it moves the stock from **“intact / staged hold” to “under review pending transaction terms.”**

Key questions before changing the rating:

- Is the transaction signed, and what is the cash/share/debt consideration?
- What is the pro-forma capital and dilution impact?
- Can Monzo's profitability be acquired without weakening NU's Brazil/Mexico credit discipline?
- Does management disclose a clear return-on-invested-capital hurdle and integration timetable?

### Conditional framework

- **No confirmed deal / no material dilution:** keep the existing position under review; do not average down solely because the price fell.
- **Large premium or equity-funded acquisition without a clear hurdle rate:** consider reducing NU toward the lower end of its portfolio range and reallocate only after comparing alternatives such as UBER, MCD or PACS.
- **Disciplined, accretive structure with transparent funding and integration milestones:** thesis can return to intact, but add only in stages after confirmation.

## Event 2 — MU FY2026 Q4 earnings on 30 September

Micron's official investor-relations notice confirms that FY2026 Q4 results and the conference call are scheduled for **30 September 2026**. The thesis card's reference point is approximately $50bn revenue, 86% gross margin and non-GAAP EPS of $31 ± $1; the central risk is that current earnings represent a cyclical peak rather than a durable HBM step-up.

Source: [Micron official earnings announcement](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Technology-to-Report-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-Results-on-September-30-2026/default.aspx), [Micron events and presentations](https://investors.micron.com/events-and-presentations/default.aspx).

### What matters more than headline EPS

1. Revenue and gross margin versus the guide, with particular attention to HBM and data-centre demand.
2. Forward pricing and supply commentary: whether tight supply persists or customers begin to defer orders.
3. Capex, free cash flow and the timing of incremental capacity.
4. The next-quarter guide and management's description of normalised—not peak—earnings power.

### Conditional framework

- **Guide-up with durable HBM/pricing evidence:** thesis reinforced; additions should still be staged because MU already represents about 18% and AVGO/NVDA add correlated risk.
- **In-line quarter but cautious guide:** thesis intact but under review; hold rather than chase or panic-sell, then reassess normalised earnings.
- **Guide-down, pricing deterioration or weak HBM demand:** thesis materially weakened; a reduction toward a lower target range is rational to consider, especially if the AI-semiconductor bucket remains above 40%.
