The company expects operations, commitments and roadmap to continue. That expectation is management's statement, not proof. At [17 November fiscal Q3 results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Sets-Conference-Call-for-Third-Quarter-Financial-Results/), test data-center sales and forward orders, Blackwell/Rubin timing, gross margin, customer concentration and the permanent-CEO process.
