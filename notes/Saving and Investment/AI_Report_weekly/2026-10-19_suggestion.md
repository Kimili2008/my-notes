## Risk controls and immediate information gaps

- **First obtain the denominator:** cash, account total, any positions outside `Portfolio_AI.md`, current trades, cost bases, tax lots, liabilities and near-term cash needs. Without these, neither actual account weights nor realized P&L can be stated, and the bands above cannot be turned into share counts.
- **Stress correlated sleeves, not isolated tickers.** For illustration only, a 30% adverse move in a sleeve occupying 40% of equities implies a 12% equity-sleeve mark change before offsets; the full-account effect depends on cash and other assets. Do not assume nominally different AI names provide independent diversification.
- **Predefine thesis invalidators.** RKLB: post-2027 Neutron slip or Space Systems growth/margin/cash deterioration. MU: sustained ASP/gross-margin/inventory deterioration. APP: Q3 miss with failed model/e-commerce validation. NU/SOFI: persistent risk-adjusted credit or funding deterioration. A generic percentage stop is not a substitute for this review.
- **Near-term event discipline:** do not take a leveraged or short position to express an inflation, Brazil-election, SOFI-earnings or APP-earnings view. Stage any eventual approved exposure changes only after the relevant result and full-account concentration are known.
