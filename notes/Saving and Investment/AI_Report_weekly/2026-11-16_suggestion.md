## Decision focus

At latest indicated 16 November prices, the disclosed equities mark at **$17,518**. MU **29.8%** and SNDK **9.1%** create **38.9% memory exposure**; adding AVGO and NVDA brings the semiconductor/AI hardware group to **57.3%**. The investor's stated 40% return aspiration and Sharpe >0.8 are objectives, not verified portfolio risk limits. First determine a tolerable *whole-account* loss budget from broker holdings and cash; then decide whether the sleeve ranges below fit it. ## Controls and decision calendar

1. **Reconcile the account.** Obtain broker shares, cash, other holdings, cost bases, FX and unsettled trades before treating any sleeve band as a whole-account allocation.
2. **Budget correlated loss.** Model MU/SNDK/NVDA/AVGO together, including a 20% common drawdown, then set the maximum tolerable *account* impact. Do not count a high card score as diversification.
3. **Sequence evidence.** NVDA Q3 on **17 November**; ROAD final results **19 November**; SNDK split-adjusted trading **20 November**; MU Fab 11E clarification and NAND/HBM pricing; NU next credit-vintage update. Rebuild event-adjusted scenarios before changing ratings or expected values.
4. **No leveraged response to a drawdown.** The disclosed equity sleeve already has substantial common-factor risk, while cash and borrowing capacity are unknown.
