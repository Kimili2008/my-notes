## Risk controls and decision gates

- **CPI/PPI (14–15 October):** avoid treating one release as a directional signal. Recheck sleeve exposures if yields gap materially, especially AI semis, APP and space.
- **Brazil runoff (25 October):** review NU size against a pre-defined maximum; do not add solely because of a favorable poll or one-day currency move.
- **Earnings:** avoid adding before major holdings report unless the expected-value case explicitly compensates for gap risk. For APP and MU, use card KPIs rather than price action as the validation test.
- **Concentration:** review the AI-semiconductor sleeve near 40% and space near 18% of listed equities. These are discussion thresholds, not mandates; account-level allocations may differ because cash and other assets are unknown.
- **Sizing:** stage any approved additions and preserve a cash buffer. Do not average down without a revalidated thesis; no leverage or shorting recommendation.
- **Replacement test:** a watchlist candidate should replace—not automatically stack on top of—overlapping exposure when it offers better risk-adjusted upside, improves factor diversification, and clears thesis-specific KPIs.

## Decision summary

The most defensible near-term posture is to hold core exposure while avoiding price-driven additions into the concentrated AI and space sleeves. The standout incremental watchlist research candidates are **ORCL** (strong card rating but still AI-capex correlated) and **UBER** (greater business-model diversification). Neither is a recommendation to buy without refreshed valuation, account cash and risk-capacity information.
