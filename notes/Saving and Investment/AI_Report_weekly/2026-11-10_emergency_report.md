# Portfolio Event Note | Rocket Lab (RKLB) Q3 2026

**10 November 2026 | 10:50 UTC | Pre-market review**  
**Event classification:** Material thesis update and post-earnings decision point. **Thesis status:** operating demand **reinforced**; per-share value and Neutron execution **under review**.

## Investment conclusion

Rocket Lab's Q3 release materially improves the evidence for demand: revenue reached **$267.8 million** (+73% year on year), backlog rose **52% sequentially to $3.57 billion**, and Q3 GAAP operating cash flow turned positive at **$7.2 million**. More than **$515 million** of new Neutron contracts took that vehicle's pre-debut backlog to **16 missions**. This does not validate Neutron technically: management still targets a first launch **no earlier than 2027**. The other decisive change for a shareholder is financing. The company says its proposed Iridium acquisition has been fully funded through an at-the-market (ATM) equity offering; nine-month gross ATM proceeds were **$3.91 billion** and common shares outstanding rose from **543.6 million** at 2025 year-end to **639.2 million** at September 2026, roughly **17.6%**. Revenue growth and cash runway improved, while dilution and integration remain central to **per-share** expected value. [Company Q3 release and statements](https://investors.rocketlabcorp.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rocket-lab-announces-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-posts).

Cash, other assets, and account equity are not disclosed, so an overall-account move above 2% cannot be substantiated. The event qualifies for this note because the results and equity financing materially change inputs to the written thesis, not because the observed position move meets the account threshold. A pre-market quote is not an execution price.

Do **not** mechanically increase the old $80 risk-adjusted reference or $85–$105 base target: its September model preceded the disclosed share issuance, pending Iridium financing, and confirmed 2027 Neutron timing. A refreshed expected-value calculation needs (1) current diluted share count including preferred conversion and any subsequent issuance, (2) Iridium purchase price and incremental cash flows, (3) backlog conversion schedule and project margin, and (4) a probability-weighted Neutron first-flight and ramp path. At the Q4 guidance midpoint, $280m of revenue would be only ~4.5% above Q3; valuation still depends heavily on later execution. The 17.6% common-share increase is a **denominator change**, not a 17.6% estimate of economic dilution from the Iridium transaction alone. ## Catalysts and conditional action framework

1. **At the U.S. open / today:** Re-mark the holding at a live executable quote. Treat the pre-market rise as a price response, not proof of improved risk-adjusted return. A rally without revised per-share value narrows the entry margin.
2. **Next filings and deal documents:** Reconcile fully diluted shares, ATM issuance timing, Iridium consideration, closing conditions, debt and integration costs. If per-share earnings power fails to improve after acquisition costs, retain **under review** despite headline backlog growth.
3. **Q4 release:** Test revenue against $270m–$290m and GAAP gross margin against 34%–36%; track Space Systems organic growth, backlog conversion, operating cash flow less capital spending, and Neutron hardware/qualification milestones. Sustained cash generation and a credible first-flight path would reinforce the thesis; renewed margin deterioration, repeated equity issuance, or a further Neutron slip would warrant a lower risk-adjusted value.
4. **Position discipline:** No automatic add or trim follows from this note. Reassess sizing only after the refreshed per-share model and full account weights are available. The September card's **0/10 rating** should be considered stale pending that work.
