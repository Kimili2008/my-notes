## Thesis and expected-value implication

Q3's reported **$2.367bn Connectivity operating income**, **13.6m Starlink consumer subscribers**, **$69 monthly ARPU**, and **$431m SI operating income** strengthen the operating case; they do not become weaker because locked holders may sell. Conversely, Q3 **$19.593bn capex** and management's reported **$35–40bn Q4 capex outlook** require a fresh capital-return model. The proposed Tesla combination remains exploratory without verified terms. The card's September **0/10** rating and **$160–175 risk-adjusted reference** are stale for post-Q3 decision-making, but neither the earnings beat nor this unlock justifies an ungrounded new target.
