# Weekly Portfolio Research Addendum — 21 September 2026

## New Information Since the Initial Draft

Asian equities opened higher on Monday after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent described Sunday’s U.S.–China discussions as successful ahead of the expected Trump–Xi meeting. South Korea’s Kospi rose 1.8%, Samsung Electronics gained 5%, SK hynix rose 1% and Taiwan’s Taiex gained 1.1%. This is a **tactical improvement in semiconductor sentiment**, not yet evidence of a durable change in export controls, tariffs or AI supply-chain policy.

The portfolio implication is asymmetric: a successful diplomatic signal can support AVGO, MU and NVDA in the short run, but those same positions remain exposed to the still-elevated 10-year Treasury yield and to any reversal in U.S.–China technology negotiations. Do not treat Monday’s futures strength as a reason to lift the AI-infrastructure sleeve above its target cap.

## Updated Positioning

- Keep the existing recommendation: no incremental leverage before Micron’s 30 September fiscal Q4 release.
- If semiconductor holdings gap higher on diplomacy alone, use the strength to check concentration and rebalance only if the combined AVGO / MU / NVDA sleeve is above target.
- The next macro risk is policy substance, not headlines: export controls, advanced-chip access, tariffs and enforcement language should be scored for direct revenue impact.
- MU remains the highest-priority catalyst. Micron has formally confirmed its 30 September earnings date; the thesis still requires HBM demand, pricing discipline, 2027 capacity economics and durable free-cash-flow conversion.
