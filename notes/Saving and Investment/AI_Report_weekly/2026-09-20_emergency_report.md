# Emergency Portfolio Report — Micron Fiscal Q4 Earnings Ahead

**Date:** 20 September 2026  
**Severity:** High for the MU position; potentially material for the overall portfolio because MU sits in the same AI-memory / semiconductor factor as AVGO and NVDA.  
**Event date:** 30 September 2026, after market close.

## Executive Alert

Micron has formally scheduled its fiscal Q4 2026 results for 30 September. The stock closed at **$1,015.80 on 18 September**, up 3.9% on the session. Current third-party consensus is approximately **$50.4–50.6bn of revenue and $31.1–31.3 of EPS**; these are market estimates, not company guidance. The last reported quarter was exceptional, and the market is now pricing a very high bar for another beat-and-raise.

This is not a thesis-break event. It is a **high-volatility decision point**: the written MU thesis depends on HBM4 volume, tight DRAM/NAND supply, durable pricing and a structurally higher mid-cycle margin. A strong headline beat without constructive 2027 commentary may not be sufficient if expectations have already moved higher.

## What the Existing Thesis Requires

The MU thesis card is reinforced by HBM4 high-volume shipments and the planned 2027 HBM4E ramp. The earnings release must now validate:

1. HBM revenue, customer qualification and forward commitments;
2. DRAM and NAND pricing, bit-demand outlook and evidence of supply discipline;
3. Gross-margin durability rather than a one-quarter peak;
4. 2027 capex, wafer / packaging capacity and expected return on incremental investment;
5. Free cash flow conversion and customer prepayment / strategic agreement quality.

The most important breaker remains a simultaneous weakening in HBM demand and conventional memory pricing, especially if Samsung or SK hynix capacity comes online faster than expected.
