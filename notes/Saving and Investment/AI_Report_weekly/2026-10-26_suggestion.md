## Risk controls and next actions for the investor to decide

1. **Reconcile the missing account data** before any target-weight implementation: actual cash and securities outside this file, current weights, cost bases, currency, tax residence and a maximum tolerable one-month drawdown. Without them, the above ranges cannot be converted into shares or orders.
2. **Choose a total correlated-risk ceiling.** The disclosed AI-semiconductor plus space cluster is about **63.4%**; a hypothetical 15% common shock would remove about **9.5% of this equity sleeve** before correlations elsewhere. Consider whether that is compatible with the investor's risk tolerance and intended horizon. Do not use leverage to repair a drawdown.
3. **Use event gates, not price-only rules.** SOFI Q3 on 27 October, FOMC on 28 October, AMZN on 29 October, APP on 4 November and RKLB on 9 November can change the evidence. For NU, observe post-election BRL/local rates and then credit/ROE; for SPCX, monitor SI customer exposure and cash economics. [Calendar and sources](2026-10-26_report.md#catalystkpi-calendar-and-decision-thresholds).

**No trade is required by this memo.** It identifies conditions under which a smaller correlated position, more liquidity, or a differentiated replacement would be worth evaluating. The user's 40% annual-return goal is aspirational and cannot substitute for a verified loss budget or expected-value calculation.
