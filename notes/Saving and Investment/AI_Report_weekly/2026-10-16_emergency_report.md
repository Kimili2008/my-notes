# Portfolio Event Note — September U.S. PPI Inflation Shock

*16 October 2026, approximately 09:55 UTC / 10:55 London. This is a follow-up to the 15 October pre-PPI note; U.S. regular trading has not opened today. Prices cited are indicative latest available quotes, not an account statement.*

## Investment conclusion

September U.S. final-demand PPI rose **1.3% month over month** and **6.3% year over year**, the fastest monthly and annual increases since 2022. The measure excluding food, energy and trade services rose **0.5% month over month** and **5.0% year over year**. Energy was the main driver of goods inflation, but services also rose 0.8%. The release strengthens the *inflation-persistence risk* already flagged after CPI; it does not establish weaker operating demand at any portfolio company. [BLS September PPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ppi.nr0.htm).

Crucially, the bond market did **not** respond with a simple higher-yield move: the U.S. Treasury's 10-year constant-maturity yield declined from **5.19% on 14 October to 5.10% on 15 October** despite the hot print. The equity reaction also moderated after early weakness. Do not assume PPI alone mechanically lowers the fair value of every growth holding; the persistence of inflation and the rates response now need to be judged together. [U.S. Treasury daily par yields](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value=2026&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve).

## Expected value and conditional action framework

**No numerical target-price change or automatic trade is warranted.** The distribution of macro outcomes has widened, while Treasury yields actually fell on the day. Before adding to a dip or trimming a position, confirm the full-account weight (including cash), the post-open price/yield response and whether direct company KPIs changed. A sustained rise in real yields coupled with broad weakness in the semiconductor and space sleeves would justify reviewing aggregate concentration; stable yields and intact company guidance would argue against treating this PPI release as a broken thesis. Do not use leverage to trade the inflation print.

The next macro and earnings checks are today's scheduled U.S. import/export-price release and late-October company results. This note does not pre-judge unreleased data. [BLS release schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/2026/).
