## Rules for adjusting the pace

- **Accelerate review** if broker excess liquidity falls materially, a maintenance notice arrives, borrowing terms change, or a core thesis suffers confirmed operating damage. This calendar cannot supersede broker deadlines. No reliable forced-liquidation price can be estimated from the information provided.
- **Do not re-expand the loan** after a planned reduction. Review the borrowing rate and actual interest every week until the balance is zero.
- **Reassess completed and remaining quantities weekly.** Record actual trades, fees, settled cash and NAV. Fractional-share support must be verified; round only after checking the dollar target and holdings cap.
- **Refresh the investment ranking before later batches.** These shares implement the current agreed risk objective; stronger or weaker company evidence can justify substituting sales, with a newly reconciled plan. Do not automatically sell a stronger thesis merely because its scheduled date arrived.
- **No automatic trades are authorized by this document.** It is a written decision framework; maintain the account's actual execution ledger separately.
