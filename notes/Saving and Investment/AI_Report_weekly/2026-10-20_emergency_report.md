## What the print must prove

The 28 September thesis card rates SPCX **0/10, medium-low confidence**, with risk-adjusted 12-month value around **$160–175** against a then-$148.68 reference. Its core proposition is that profitable Connectivity can finance the Space and AI options without permanent deterioration in per-share value. In Q2, Connectivity revenue was **$4.291 billion** and operating income **$1.656 billion**; Starlink had **12.0 million subscribers** and **$66 monthly ARPU**. AI produced **$2.561 billion** of revenue but a **$1.257 billion operating loss** and **$15.828 billion of capex**. Those are the baselines, not Q3 estimates. [SpaceX Q2 SEC-filed results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026052515/earningsreleaseq22608042.htm).

| Segment / risk | Q3 evidence to extract | Thesis implication |
|---|---|---|
| Connectivity / Starlink | Net subscriber adds; ARPU versus $66; enterprise/government mix; operating margin versus Q2 ~38.6% | Subscriber growth plus resilient unit economics **reinforces** the cash engine. A material margin drop or ARPU below the card's $60 breaker warrants review. |
| AI | Revenue conversion from contracted sales; operating loss versus Q2 $1.257bn; capex versus Q2 $15.828bn; utilization and financing | Revenue growth without improving operating economics or capex discipline does **not** prove attractive returns. Rising capex alongside slowing AI growth would weaken the card. |
| Space / Starship | Launch and V3 deployment progress; reuse milestones; Space operating loss and capex | Separate demonstrated milestones from aspirational cost-reduction claims. Major technical or regulatory setbacks could impair both Space and Starlink growth assumptions. |
| Consolidated funding | Operating cash flow, free cash flow, debt, share issuance, related-party terms and full-year outlook | The highest-conviction risk is whether Starlink cash generation remains sufficient relative to AI/Starship reinvestment and dilution. |

## Conditional action framework

- **Reinforcing print:** Connectivity profit and unit economics hold, AI loss/capex trajectory becomes more credible, and funding remains controlled. Re-estimate the card's segment values and risk discount before considering any change; a favorable headline alone is insufficient.
- **Mixed print:** Subscriber/revenue growth remains strong but ARPU, operating profit or cash conversion weakens. Keep the thesis *under review* until the webcast clarifies drivers; avoid averaging down merely because of an opening-price decline.
- **Adverse print:** Connectivity margin falls toward/below **30%**, ARPU falls below **$60** with sharply slower subscriber growth, AI loss widens while capex rises, or debt/dilution materially expands. Treat these as possible thesis-breaker evidence and reassess position risk after the filing and call, not from a single adjusted-EBITDA figure.
