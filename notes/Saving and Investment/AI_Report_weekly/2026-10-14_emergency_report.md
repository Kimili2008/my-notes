# Portfolio Event Note — U.S. CPI, Semiconductor Supply Chain and Concentration Review

*14 October 2026, approximately 8:35 a.m. ET | Early release assessment; U.S. cash equities had not opened. Prices below are 13 October closing/late quotes and do not reflect today's CPI reaction.*

## Investment conclusion

September U.S. CPI rose **0.6% month over month and 3.7% year over year**, versus **0.4% and 3.4%** in August. Core CPI rose **0.3% month over month and 2.6% year over year**, versus **0.3% and 2.4%** in August. The headline monthly rise matched the 0.6% forecast cited by AP, while the annual headline reading was 0.1 percentage point above its 3.6% survey forecast; core monthly inflation was 0.1 point above its 0.2% forecast. Gasoline (+9.1% month over month) accounted for more than half the headline rise, but shelter also accelerated to +0.4% and core services excluding energy rose +0.4%. This is a **rates and valuation risk**, not evidence that any portfolio company's operating thesis has failed. [BLS September CPI release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm); [AP pre-release consensus](https://apnews.com/article/3985019a54c82421e6112fb57baba048)

ASML separately reported record Q3 sales of **€11.7 billion**, a **57.5%** gross margin, and customer demand forecasts strong enough that its current 2027–28 equipment supply plan may not fully meet demand. Its Q4 sales outlook is **€14.2–15.2 billion**. This supports the broad AI/semiconductor investment cycle, but it is not direct confirmation of MU's memory selling prices, NVDA's customer demand, or AVGO's custom-silicon margins. Planned capacity expansion could eventually loosen the memory supply constraint. [ASML Q3 release](https://www.investor.asml.com/news-releases/news-release-details/asml-reports-eu11.7-billion-total-net-sales-and-eu5.0-billion-net-income-q3-2026)

The disclosed ten-stock equity subtotal marked to approximately **$9,159.01** at 13 October prices, down **3.3%** from the static-share mark used in Monday's report. This is an indicative change in the disclosed equities, **not** the actual account return: cash, other assets, intraday trades and cost bases are unknown. The move is large enough to warrant a concentration review even though the recent share-price declines alone do not break a thesis.

## Expected-value and risk assessment

- **No numerical target-price revision yet.** One macro print changes the probability of a higher-for-longer rate path, but a responsible expected-value update also needs post-release yield pricing and company earnings/cash-flow evidence. The ASML read-through is positive for sector investment but ambiguous for future memory supply. Keep the existing card values as historical references, not live fair values.
- **Risk asymmetry:** an adverse rates reaction can hit several correlated sleeves simultaneously. The 42.4% semiconductor and 22.4% space weights are measured against listed equities only; actual account exposure may be lower or higher once cash and other positions are known.
- **Thesis status:** MU **reinforced/cyclical**; AVGO/NVDA **intact**; APP **intact, under review**; all other holdings retain Monday's status pending new direct company evidence. Neither today's CPI nor ASML release alone supports a “broken” designation.
