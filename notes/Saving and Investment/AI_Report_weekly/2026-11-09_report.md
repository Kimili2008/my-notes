## Company developments, valuation implications and quarterly tests

**MU | Under review, not broken.** Its [FY2026 Q4 release](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Record-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2026-Results/default.aspx) reported $54.23 billion revenue, 87.0% non-GAAP gross margin and FY2027 Q1 guidance of $61.5 billion ± $1.5 billion revenue and approximately 86.25% non-GAAP gross margin. The newer issue is a [reported pause of a $3–4.5 billion Taoyuan HBM/DRAM plant previously slated for 2028](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/824699), linked to labor negotiations. Micron says current Taiwan operations continue normally but has not directly confirmed the new plant's status; separate current production disruption is **not established**. On Friday the Taoyuan parties reportedly agreed to develop a bonus-calculation framework and schedule further talks, **not a final settlement**. [Taiwan labor update](https://meridianews.org/articles/cmw5pakie00hwq3izle7tfe2a). The rewritten [card](../stocks%20Evaluation/MU_2026-09-28.md) has judgmental $560/$1,194/$1,814 bear/base/bull prices and a ~$1,096 probability-weighted value **at its 4 November assumptions**.
