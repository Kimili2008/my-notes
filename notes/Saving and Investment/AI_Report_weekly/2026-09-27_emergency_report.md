# Emergency Portfolio Report — Three Trading Days to Micron Earnings

**Date:** 27 September 2026  
**Catalyst:** Micron fiscal Q4 2026 earnings, 30 September after the close / 4:30 p.m. ET call.  
**Status:** High event risk; MU thesis remains intact but the bar has risen with the share price.

## Executive Alert

Micron remains scheduled to report fiscal Q4 on 30 September. The latest market snapshot shows MU around **$1,082**, versus approximately $1,016 in the prior research note. The semiconductor index also recovered on Friday. This price strength is not itself evidence that the earnings thesis has improved; it increases the risk of a “good results, sell the news” reaction if guidance merely meets already elevated expectations.

The company’s latest disclosed operating evidence remains strong: Q3 adjusted free cash flow was $18.3bn, Cloud Memory gross margin was 83%, and Core Data Center gross margin was 87%. The upcoming release must establish that these economics are durable rather than peak-cycle outcomes.

## Decision Framework

- **Reinforce:** HBM4 volumes and customer commitments rise; DRAM/NAND pricing remains tight; Q1 and 2027 guidance exceed the market’s already elevated expectations; capex remains economically disciplined.
- **Mixed:** Revenue / EPS beat, but 2027 margin, supply or capex commentary is cautious. Do not chase; reassess position size after the call.
- **Break risk:** HBM demand slows, pricing rolls over, supply expands faster than demand, or management guides materially below expectations. Re-underwrite the full memory thesis before adding.

## Portfolio Risk Controls

1. No new leverage before the print. A strong long-term thesis does not eliminate overnight gap risk.
2. Measure MU together with AVGO and NVDA as one correlated AI-infrastructure sleeve.
3. If MU is above its intended weight, consider reducing excess gross exposure before the event rather than relying on a binary outcome.
4. Do not interpret a pre-earnings rally as confirmation; require post-earnings guidance and cash-flow evidence.
