# Emergency Portfolio Report — 30 September 2026

*Pre-earnings decision note: Micron Technology (MU) reports FY2026 Q4 after the US close today.*

## Why this matters

MU is approximately **18% of the disclosed portfolio**. At the latest available quote of roughly **$1,065**, the position is worth about **$1,683** before cash, fees and taxes. A 10% post-earnings move would therefore change the account by approximately **1.8 percentage points** before any read-through to AVGO or NVDA. A correlated 5% move in those two holdings would take the combined account impact above 3%.

Micron's official prior guide was approximately **$50bn revenue ±$1bn, 86% gross margin and $31 ±$1 non-GAAP EPS**. Street estimates are clustered around $50–51bn revenue and $31–31.5 EPS. A small headline beat may therefore be insufficient if forward commentary is cautious. [Micron official guide](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Record-Results-for-the-Third-Quarter-of-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx), [Micron earnings schedule](https://investors.micron.com/events-and-presentations/default.aspx)

## What to read beyond headline EPS

1. **HBM and data-centre demand:** shipment volumes, customer commitments, HBM4 qualification and the durability of pricing.
2. **DRAM/NAND pricing:** whether tight supply extends beyond HBM or whether conventional memory is beginning to soften.
3. **Gross margin quality:** whether approximately 86% is sustainable, versus a one-quarter mix or spot-price peak.
4. **FY2027 guide and language:** management's view of normalised earnings power matters more than the quarter just completed.
5. **Capex and free cash flow:** whether capacity investment is translating into returns rather than merely increasing the asset base.

## Scenario framework

| Result | Thesis interpretation | Portfolio response framework |
|---|---|---|
| Beat plus durable FY2027 guide, stable HBM/pricing | Reinforced | Hold; additions only in stages because the AI-semiconductor bucket is already highly concentrated |
| In-line quarter, cautious forward language | Intact but under review | Avoid chasing; reassess normalised earnings and valuation after the call |
| Guide-down, pricing deterioration or HBM delay | Materially weakened | Consider reducing exposure toward a pre-set ceiling; reassess AVGO/NVDA correlation at the same time |

## Bottom line

The main risk is not that MU's reported quarter is suddenly poor. It is that a very strong quarter fails to prove that current earnings are durable rather than cyclical. Do not use leverage into the release. The thesis should be updated after the call using forward demand, pricing, capex and cash flow—not the EPS headline alone.
