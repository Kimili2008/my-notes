## Evidence behind the sector move

**PC weakness versus data-center strength.** [Intel's 29 October results](https://www.intc.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1786/intel-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results) guided Q4 revenue to **$15.0–16.0 billion**, below its $16.5 billion Q3, and management expected the PC slowdown to continue into at least H1 2027. Its data-center revenue nevertheless grew sharply. The weak PC outlook is relevant to commodity DRAM/NAND demand but should not be mapped mechanically onto HBM demand.

**Fresh AI demand evidence remains positive.** [AMD's 3 November filing](https://ir.amd.com/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000002488-26-000188/q32026991.htm) reported Q3 data-center revenue of **$8.5 billion, up 97% year over year**, and Q4 group revenue guidance near **$17 billion ±3%**. [Micron's latest reported quarter](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Record-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2026-Results/default.aspx) delivered **$54.23 billion** revenue and **87.0% non-GAAP gross margin**; its FY2027 Q1 guidance was **$61.5 billion ± $1.5 billion** revenue and approximately **86.25%** non-GAAP gross margin. These figures support the written MU demand thesis but leave substantial risk that peak margins and pricing are not sustainable.

**Sandisk is a related, distinct exposure.** Its [29 October fiscal Q1 release](https://investor.sandisk.com/news-releases/news-release-details/sandisk-reports-fiscal-first-quarter-2027-financial-results) reported **$11.0 billion** revenue, up 23% quarter over quarter, and guided fiscal Q2 to **$12.2–12.6 billion**. It also announced an **8-for-1 split**, with post-split trading expected on 20 November. That split changes share count and per-share price, not economic ownership. SNDK's NAND and SSD economics overlap the memory cycle but are not identical to MU's HBM/DRAM exposure. The current thesis directory has **no SNDK card**, so there is no written valuation, monitoring KPI or invalidation threshold for this 10% sleeve position.
