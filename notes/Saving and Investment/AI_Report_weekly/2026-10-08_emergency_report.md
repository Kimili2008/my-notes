Participants cited persistent inflation, upside energy/tariff risks and AI-related demand; several viewed policy as not restrictive or only mildly restrictive. This is a more hawkish signal than the weak September payroll report alone would suggest. [Federal Reserve FOMC minutes](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcminutes20260916.htm)

The runoff between Flávio Bolsonaro and Lula is scheduled for **25 October**. ## Expected-value / risk implications

The Fed minutes do not break a company thesis by themselves, but they challenge the portfolio's shared assumption that rates will soon ease and support high-duration valuations. No defensible per-share valuation change can be calculated solely from the minutes. The portfolio's concentrated sleeves mean a further rate-driven repricing could plausibly move overall account value by more than 2%; total-account weights remain unknown.

For NU, the first-round result is supportive of immediate market sentiment but is not a resolved political outcome. It is premature to raise expected earnings or intrinsic value without new evidence on policy, currency, NIM and credit quality. A runoff reversal could retrace part of the election premium.

- Do not sell or buy solely on the minutes. Reassess after September CPI (14 October) and before/after the 27–28 October FOMC meeting using the long-yield trend and company-level forward guidance.
- Avoid adding leverage to a routine dip; the book already carries significant correlated duration risk and actual cash is unknown.
- If the AI-semiconductor sleeve remains above a preselected ceiling (illustrative 40%), a measured rebalance can be considered without declaring any individual thesis broken.
- For NU, avoid chasing the gap-up. Revisit position size if it exceeds a chosen cap; reassess BRL/risk premium after 25 October and credit/NIM metrics at the next company update.
- If yields materially ease and company forward estimates remain firm, the thesis may remain intact despite volatility; if CPI reaccelerates and yields rise while estimates weaken, reduce concentration before adding to any correlated growth name.

This is a conditional risk framework, not a trade instruction. Cash, cost basis, tax lots and total-account exposure are unavailable.
