## Investment call

**Status: operating thesis reinforced; cycle valuation under review. Standalone rating: +4/10; confidence: medium-low.** The probability-weighted 12-month scenario value below is approximately **$1,096 per share**, about **37%** above the reference quote. That is a judgmental scenario model, not a DCF, forecast or instruction to increase a position. At approximately 30% of disclosed equities, MU's portfolio risk is much larger than its stand-alone score conveys. The earlier +3/10 card and its pre-results prices are superseded by this card. [Rating rubric](../rating%20criteria.md).

**Core thesis.** AI infrastructure increases demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and server DRAM while qualification, packaging and cleanroom lead times constrain supply. Micron may earn unusually high cash profits for longer than in a conventional PC-led memory cycle. The thesis fails if customer orders do not turn into shipments and cash, or if new capacity and weaker end demand drive sustained ASP and margin compression. No company filing reviewed today establishes that failure. [FY2026 results](https://investors.micron.com/news/press-release/2026/Micron-Technology-Inc--Reports-Record-Fiscal-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2026-Results/default.aspx); [FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000023/mu-20260903.htm).

## Business economics and evidence

Micron sells DRAM, HBM and NAND. HBM and server memory are the highest-conviction demand engines; consumer memory remains more exposed to PC/device cycles. Product qualification and capital intensity create barriers, but strong industry profits can still finance an eventual supply response.

## 12-month scenario valuation

**Method:** illustrative FY2027 **GAAP** revenue × assumed net margin ÷ 1.143B diluted shares = scenario EPS; multiply by a cycle-sensitive P/E. Revenue, margin, multiple and probabilities are analyst assumptions, not management guidance or statistically calibrated estimates. FY2026 reported GAAP net margin was about 64%, and fiscal Q1 2027 revenue guidance annualizes above $240B; the base case deliberately assumes a lower full-year revenue run rate rather than extrapolating one strong quarter. Targets exclude dividends and any future share-count change.

| Scenario | Probability | FY2027 revenue | Net margin | EPS | P/E | 12-month price | Thesis condition |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Bear | 35% | $160B | 50% | ~$70 | 8× | **~$560** | ASP and margin normalize quickly; customer commitments convert slowly. |
| Base | 45% | $210B | 65% | ~$119 | 10× | **~$1,194** | HBM/server demand holds, but peak Q1 conditions fade. |
| Bull | 20% | $240B | 72% | ~$151 | 12× | **~$1,814** | Supply remains tight and premium mix sustains near-peak profitability. |

Probability-weighted price: **~$1,096** (35% × $560 + 45% × $1,194 + 20% × $1,814). Versus $801.54, modeled upside is **~37%**, placing it in the rubric's **+4** band. The bear case is **~30% below** the quote. The spread is large because a modest change in normalized margin or the multiple moves equity value substantially. A ten percentage-point net-margin change at $210B revenue changes EPS by roughly $18.4 and, at 10×, value by about **$184 per share**. This model should be rebuilt when new guidance arrives.

## KPI dashboard and falsification rules

| Watch item | Reinforces thesis | Requires review / potential break |
|---|---|---|
| HBM and server DRAM orders | Qualified demand turns into shipments and paid revenue | Cancellations, qualification delays or inventory accumulation across customers |
| DRAM/NAND ASP and bit shipments | Mix and price sustain revenue without channel stuffing | Sequential ASP declines combined with flat/falling bits for two quarters |
| Gross margin | Near-term performance broadly supports ~86.25% non-GAAP fiscal Q1 guidance | Material miss with weaker orders; sustained decline toward ~70% challenges the extended-cycle case |
| Cash conversion | Operating cash and FCF remain strong after capex | Receivables/inventory rise while cash generation deteriorates |
| Capacity discipline | New cleanrooms and HBM conversions earn attractive returns | New industry supply arrives ahead of contracted demand |
| Capital allocation | Investment converts into per-share cash earnings | Persistent capex growth without revenue/cash returns |

A **single down day** changes neither rating nor status. A major miss against fiscal Q1 guidance would move the card to *under review*. Sustained ASP/margin decline plus order cancellation or poor cash conversion would make the central thesis *broken*. The thresholds are research triggers, not mechanical trading rules.
